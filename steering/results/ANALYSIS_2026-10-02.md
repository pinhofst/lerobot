MolmoAct2 (`checkpoints/MolmoAct2-SO100_101-LeRobot-1cam`), zero-shot on the SO-101, one wrist camera,
RTC inference, started from the median training pose. 21 run folders. One (17:38:47) has no ticks.
That leaves 20 analysed runs: 1 with a 4° cap and 19 with a 6° cap.

**Neutral prompts:**

**What this does and does not show:**
- **Solid:** the outcomes in the table above.
- **Preliminary:**
  - Any colour-following claim. It rests on n = 3, with "red prompt, red on the right" never run and
    "green prompt, green on the left" run only once.

**What would settle it:** a counterbalanced block at a fixed start pose. That means both layouts × red /
green / neutral prompts, at least 3 runs each, in random order, labelled blind.

## 2. Grasp failures

**Single-pen runs (9 red, 3 green; it was green in no_color_3, _9 and _10, not red):**
- Only 2 grasps in 12 runs: no_color_2 at 21.9 s and no_color_8 at 27.5 s.
- Those came after 4 and 6 empty closes. There were 35 empty closes in total.

**Gripper evidence:**
- On a pen the measured gripper stalls at **6.3–10.4** while the policy commands about 0.
- An empty close goes to **0.1–0.6**.
- The policy does reopen and retry, about every 3–5 s.

**Height evidence:**
- Grasps start with the tip computed at −0.5 to −1.1 cm (base_link z). Empty closes happen at
  −0.2 to −0.6 cm, the same depth.
- So the misses are not obviously "too high". In the wrist view the pen sits between the jaw tips, so
  it is probably beyond or below the fingertips. A wrist camera cannot show that depth.

**After a grasp:**
- Lifts reached 7.8 cm (red_2_pens_1, released in the air), 10.2 cm (green_2_pens_1, slipped),
  14.3 cm (no_color_2, slipped) and 9.0 cm (no_color_8, still held when the run ended).
- Two grasps lost the pen at the table (grip-lost).

**Tip below the assumed table:**
- Runs that reach the table compute a tip minimum of **−0.1 to −1.6 cm**, against the assumed table at
  −0.24 cm (`runs.csv`, `tip_min_z_m`).
- In press-down episodes (no_color_4, no_color_10, 2_pens_3) the tip holds at −1.4 to −1.5 cm and the
  mat visibly dents. So a few mm are real compression. The remaining ~1 cm is likely a calibration or
  mounting offset. Check it with table marks before trusting FK heights.

**Other patterns:**
- **Retract/park:** the policy commands lift −96° and elbow 84°, the clamp bounds of the trained range.
  It happens after misses (16:11 run, no_color_1), after a drop (no_color_2), or right at the start
  (2_pens_1, 2_pens_5). It looks like the policy's end-of-episode pose.
- **Hover/realign:** at least 6 s with the gripper open and no grasp, in 9 runs. In 2_pens_2 and 2_pens_4
  the pan and/or wrist_roll joints were commanded at their trained-range bounds for at least 2 s.

## 3. 4° vs 6° cap

**Not testable from these data.** There is a single 4° run (16:11). It was also the only one with
2 fps frames, and it came before the 6° runs.
- It clipped 40.5% of ticks, had 3 empty closes, then parked at 10 s.
- The 6° runs clipped 5–57% of ticks.
- The first run of the day (15:44, 3° cap, sync, folded start, not recorded) never left rest (RUNLOG).

What the data do show: at 6° most clipping is caused by the command jumping at chunk switches
(section 4), not by the cap blocking a smooth plan. A within-session A/B on the same scenes would be
needed.

## 4. Plan consistency (RTC)

**Method.** Each new chunk is compared with the chunk it replaced, over the steps both plan for the
same ticks (16–19 of 30).
- **Alignment:** recovered by matching each tick's command to the chunk rows (100% of ticks match, to
  < 0.001°), in all 20 runs. No run has merge data yet.
- **Validation:** the guided steps agree best at the chosen offset in 95–100% of chunk pairs.
- **The plot heuristic is off:** it uses `inference_delay`, which is 1–2 steps more than the 11–14
  steps actually dropped.
- **Outputs:** `plots/plan_consistency.png` per run; `results/plan_consistency_summary.csv` and `.png`
  across runs.

**Results:**
- **RTC guidance works only where it does not matter.**
  - The first 10 steps of each new chunk match the old plan to **0.02–0.24°** RMS.
  - Those are almost exactly the dropped steps.
  - The unguided steps differ by **0.3–6.0°** RMS (run medians), **2–20 mm** at the tip.
  - The executed command jumps at every switch (wrist_roll seam median 0.2–7.1° per run; 8.6° in cap6_1 hover windows).
- **Hovering is indecision, not a tracking failure.**
  - In all 8 runs with both hover and other moving windows, hover disagreement is higher, about 1.8×
    on median.
  - In median_rtc_cap6_1 (the long hover with wrist_roll oscillation):
    - unguided disagreement 6.5° vs 4.3°; tip 23.5 vs 14.5 mm;
    - wrist_roll seam jumps 8.6° vs 4.4°, single revisions up to 38°;
    - successive revisions flip direction 64% of the time.
  - The executed wrist_roll command is a staircase of these jumps. A 6° cap clips each jump, giving
    a median 47% of ticks per hover window clipped on some arm joint, and a 4.7° tracking error (all-arm RMS; wrist_roll alone 6.0°).
  - Where the command is smooth, the measured joint follows it within a few ticks.
  - So the oscillation comes from successive plans disagreeing, amplified by the cap. It is not the arm
    failing to follow a consistent plan.
- **Parked runs are the most consistent** (0.3°). The parked plan never changes.

**Preliminary:** the hover labels come from the machine labeller; direction flips are 60–77% in most
runs, hover or not.

## 5. Control-loop health

- **Loop rate:** 29.7–29.9 Hz effective in all 20 runs.
- **Inference:** median 377–463 ms per chunk, max 786–926 ms; 11–14 steps dropped per chunk.
- **Display:** the two display-on runs (cap6_1, no_color_1) had the slowest medians, 463 and 453 ms,
  against 377–424 ms with the display off. That suggests 40–60 ms per chunk. Only 2 runs, both early in
  the session: preliminary. Keep the display off.
- **Episode length:** 7 runs were stopped before 30 s (8.5–20 s), so episode length is not uniform.
  Note this when computing null rates.

## 6. Next experiments, in priority order

1. **Counterbalanced two-pen block** (fixed start pose):
   - Both layouts × red / green / neutral prompts, at least 3 runs each, in random order.
   - Must include red-on-the-right with the red prompt (never run) and more green-on-the-left.
   - The only test of colour-following vs position.
2. **Third-person camera and blind human labelling** by the written rule. Needed for "first sustained
   contact", pushes, and the depth question behind the empty closes.
3. **RTC A/B on the same scenes:**
   - `--inference.rtc.execution_horizon` 10 vs about 20, so that guidance covers the executed steps.
   - Measure seam jumps, hover time and clipping.
   - Record the merge data (rollout.py patch c).
4. **FK height check** against table marks, then a grasp-depth check: does closing 1 cm lower or later
   turn empty closes into grasps?
5. **Within-session cap A/B** (4° vs 6°, same scenes and start pose), display off.
6. **Frame-level follow-up of the scenes where parking came first** (2_pens_1, 2_pens_5), using the
   offline prompt test on their first frames (`SUMMARY.md` flags no_color_1/2 as outliers).
